# Receiving MARSH managed exchange in the original Dimension

Status: source contract and migration record, 2026-10-31. This record belongs to
`red-cup-engineering/modeled-union-dimension`.

## Required relationship and scope

An actual requester account raises grounded Work from the original induction
carrier. A provider account offers an existing Cell. The requester selects one
exact Offer, the provider submits one result, and the requester separately
receives that result. The Dimension reconstructs this relationship from a
complete authenticated MARSH history and exposes only current, context-bound
candidate actions to its injected actuator.

The machine guarantees allocated here are history-scope separation, immutable
snapshots, carrier/handle separation, exact role and lifecycle joins, resource
sequence continuity, action identity, canonical receipt consistency, and Work
projection. The Ethereum owner supplies the authenticated ABI/CBOR projection,
deployment-code observation, canonical-block observation and actual account
writer. An injected authority admission must revalidate current UCAN and
ERC-1271 authority at every execution. Readiness in a model does not grant that
authority, and chain success does not establish external Work quality.

## Profiles and identities

The managed profile is `marsh:exchange:v1`; its profile ID is SHA-256 of the
canonical CBOR text term, not a typed SEN envelope. The legacy six-event profile
is labeled `legacy:semiotic-exchange:v1`. An absent profile on historical input
selects the legacy path. An empty managed model must explicitly select the
managed profile. Mixed streams and managed `DemandSettled` refuse.

A carrier's exact bytes, its SHA-256/NI, a governed resource handle, an account,
and a chain occurrence are distinct coordinates. `demandBytes32` from the
original producer remains a carrier digest. It never becomes the governed
Demand handle. The latter is witnessed by the authenticated native admission.
A pre-chain induced candidate exposes the carrier digest and full raise
arguments; it does not invent a witnessed handle.

Generic induction receipts lack a requester coordinate. A managed model binds
one to the sole witnessed Demand matching carrier, grounding and wanted law.
If equal material has multiple controller-bound handles, the separate
`demandInductionBindings: [{receiptNi, demand}]` relation must disambiguate it.
That relation does not modify the original producer receipt. Capability-repair
induction already carries its canonical raiser and binds that account as well.

## Canonical carrier admission

The original v1 `createCarrierAdmissionReceipt` remains supported. It identifies
the original `{type,value}` wrapper, which existing Work carriers use.

The same function now accepts `canonicalBytes` and `contentNi` for v2 admission.
It asks Computer Data to admit the exact bytes and token under
`semiotic-exchange.<type>`, requires the decoded value to equal `carrier.value`,
and stores an immutable hexadecimal representation. This admits the original
induction producer's unwrapped typed record without changing its bytes or NI.
Both v1 and v2 receipts are canonically checked again on model construction.
Protocol-verification receipt references remain supplied evidence; a reference
alone is not a cryptographic verification implemented by this module.

## Replay and Work

Managed normalized events carry profile, profileId, canonical CAIP-2 chainId,
lowercase exchange address, semantic chainPosition, exact adjacent
nativePosition and resourceState. Block numbers and sequences are decimal
strings; transaction/log indices are safe nonnegative integers. Hashes and
accounts use their canonical lowercase forms.

The model checks strict native/semantic order, consistent block and transaction
occurrences, the semantic/native resource and kind, immutable controller/kind,
admission sequence one with a zero predecessor, and exact prior head/sequence
plus one for transitions. It retains a detached snapshot with immutable
carrierHex instead of exposing mutable byte arrays. When carrier bytes or hex
are supplied, it checks their NI and digest itself. The authenticated Ethereum
reader remains responsible for canonical governed identity, state/evidence
CBOR hashes, deployed code, and block/finality observations.

Demand lifecycle is `Open → Selected → Submitted → Received`. An Offer must
join the existing open Demand to an active Cell under the exact wanted law.
Selection must name that exact Offer/Cell and requester. Retirement prevents
new offers or selection but preserves submission of already selected Work.
Only the selected Cell controller can submit; only the Demand requester can
receive the exact selected Offer and submitted result. Submission and receiving
are separate events and separate locus fields.

Managed Work remains engaged after submission, even with a positive provider
completion receipt. Work and its ancestors settle only after requester
receiving and a completed provider receipt naming the selected Offer and exact
result. A valid report about an unselected Offer or another result remains
visible without closing Work. Refusal evidence and simultaneous positive and
negative support remain visible. Legacy provider settlement is explicitly
labeled as such; it is never substituted for managed receiving.

## Actions and authority

Each managed candidate includes the actual hub-calling account, profile,
chain/deployment, complete history NI, last semantic occurrence checkpoint,
relevant current resource states, operation, exact joins and observed sequences.
`actionIdentity` is the exact `RestrictedModeledUnionAction` value identified by
Computer Data under `modeled-union-dimension.RestrictedModeledUnionAction` with
its empty witness root. The action ID is that NI. `actionDigest` extracts the
SHA-256 digest of the same canonical bytes from the NI; it does not hash NI text.

Offer, submission and receiving are parameterized candidates with
`carrierRequired: true` and exact fixed-prefix args. The eventual carrier is
supplied separately to `actuate(actionId, actor, input)` and passed to the
actuator. Its full encoded call must be admitted at execution and signed by the
actual account together with the action digest. No automatic sequence refresh,
carrier fill, retry or replay of an uncertain effect is provided.

Readiness requires all relevant carrier admissions, an actuator, and a matching
canonical UCAN/controller-binding receipt pair. The account and its human or
agent principals remain distinct:

- `binding.account` is the candidate's actual EVM account; `binding.chain` is its
  CAIP-2 chain.
- Capability `with` and binding resource are `did:pkh:<chainId>:<account>`.
- UCAN controller and actor remain the original distinct did:key principals.
- Common abilities keep their existing names; the new abilities are
  `semiotic-exchange/submit-demand-result` and
  `semiotic-exchange/receive-demand`.
- Both receipts must agree on principal pair, invocation/delegation, canonical
  capability NI and the complete capability. Binding `rmnSemanticDigest` must
  equal the candidate's action digest.
- Capability `nb` contains flat primitive `marshProfile`, `marshExchange` and
  `marshActionId`. Extra caveats remain subject to the original verifier.

Actual signed UCAN 0.1.2 receiving showed that its existing default attenuation
relation cannot preserve a newly nested object caveat across decoded delegation
identity. The initial nested `nb.marsh` design was therefore replaced by these
three flat fields before acceptance. This preserves the same bound meaning
using the actual verifier. The previous design/test receipts remain identified
as superseded; no verifier was relaxed or patched in an installed supplier.

UCAN receipt and capability identity use the original
`animating.ucan-semantic-material` domain. Binding receipt identity retains
`ethereum.enterprise-account-controller-binding`. The Ethereum owner corrected
its capability projection to receive the UCAN-owned domain; it did not change
the binding receipt's own identity owner. Canonical receipt consistency here
does not replace current signature, revocation, policy, nonce or ERC-1271
observation by the actual authority provider.
